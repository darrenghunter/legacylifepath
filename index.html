About the Coach

I've been where you are, and found my way through.

I grew up the youngest of four in a single-parent home with little money. I've served in the military, built a career in the energy sector, and faced loss, major setbacks and a poor self-image along the way.

Today I have a thriving partnership, a close relationship with my family and a life I'm proud of. That journey taught me that lasting change starts with understanding yourself, then taking steady, practical action.

My focus is helping you find inner peace and happiness, understand your limiting beliefs, and build the confidence to move forward. I also coach frontline leaders and professionals in the energy sector.

My approach

A holistic, bottom-up method that connects the deep origins of your behavior with the daily actions that sustain it, grounded in mindfulness.

Cognitive Behavioral Coaching

Spot unhelpful thought patterns and replace them with ones that serve you.

Socratic Questioning

Thought-provoking questions that help you reach your own answers.

Ontological Coaching

Explore who you are being, and how that shapes what you do.

Choice Theory

Take ownership of your choices and the relationships that matter most.
